# MediRead

## Register
product

## Platform
web

## Users
Older adults, people with visual impairments, and people who struggle with medicine-label text.

## Product Purpose
Point, scan, review visible label text, and listen in English or Hindi. A local hackathon prototype with simulated OCR, browser speech, and clearly marked samples.

## Positioning
Make visible medicine-label information easier to access, with transparent uncertainty.

## Brand Personality
Calm, clear, reassuring. The supplied Code blooded deck establishes deep green, mint accents, white typography, and rounded controls.

## Anti-references
The approved brief excludes generic admin dashboards and unsupported medical advice.

## Design Principles
Make the next action obvious. Keep extracted evidence editable. Never invent missing label fields. Keep sample and manual paths available when OCR fails.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Large controls, high contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, English and Hindi speech. Explain missing voice support honestly.
